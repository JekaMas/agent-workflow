# Design

## Ownership
Shared operation templates, rules, delivery, verification and language procedures belong here. Main reusable specifications belong in openspec/specs; future changes to them begin here. Consumer changes record local adaptation and evidence, not a second generic specification owner.

## Extraction
Move reusable bodies to verification-design, sdd-go and sdd-rust references. Keep references conditional. Preserve local reference paths as tracked relative links where content is wholly generic; use thin adapters for mixed content. Preserve consumer SDK errors, signing, toolchain/commands, assertion conventions, live gates and campaign approvals. No new role, lifecycle, tool or approval gate.

## History
The consumer portable-workflow delta is retained as migration provenance. Its historical proposals, tasks, captures and rehearsals are not runtime procedures and do not become public defaults. Link the shared authoritative specification for future maintenance. Do not copy private historical evidence into the public repository.

## Evidence
Use reference-graph checks, native spec validation, shared fixtures and consumer packaging/routes/publication checks. Inspect source-to-destination preservation. These checks establish wiring and structure, not fresh-agent behavioral effectiveness or product correctness. Preserve negative fixtures for missing references. No subagents or hosted inference.

## Deferred
Debugging/conflict extraction and packaging policy changes remain separate decisions in the consumer ownership review. They are not silently included in this extraction.

The transferred baselines also include adaptive-development-workflow (all general lifecycle requirements, excluding product E2E lanes) and assurance-routing (review modes, configured API review, claim limits and maintenance entrypoint). Consumer-specific native generator/target qualification and check selectors remain consumer obligations.
