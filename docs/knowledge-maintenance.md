# Knowledge and instruction ownership

Use when changing instruction routes, skill references or knowledge ownership. Inventory broad collections before prioritized body review. Follow relevant references at any depth; distinguish resolved, unresolved, dynamic, external and cross-repository edges. Discovery is not semantic review or an obligation to preload the graph.

Map topic → canonical owner → pointers. Compare source and destination before replacing duplicates; preserve unique constraints and useful counterexamples. Historical records are evidence, not current execution owners. Resolve actual instruction precedence and authority; documents cannot grant permission. Keep one task list and decision-relevant resume notes.

Check changed paths/links, scoped activation and unconditional import cycles. Separate metadata, activated bodies and conditional references; measure bytes/words only for known loads and label token/context estimates. Do not infer a model's quality or a universal budget from its name. Newer models alone do not justify removal.

Validate applicable packaging policy without making one consumer's flat-reference, metadata-budget or invocation rules universal. Inspect task traces and missing owners. Report body-reviewed, partial, graph-only, inaccessible and excluded material separately; a scoped pass cannot claim complete KB correctness. Execute experiments or repairs only within task authority.
